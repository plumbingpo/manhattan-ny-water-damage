Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
